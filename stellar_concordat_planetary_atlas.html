Jump Point Notes

Beacon codes follow Harmonium standard: [SYSTEM ABBREV]-B[#] for major systems, T[#] for trade route hubs. Frontier coordinate format [SYS]-[coord] requires an active nav database subscription — operating without one in frontier space is illegal and dangerous. Locations marked Unmapped require black-market navigation data (₢500–15,000 depending on age and source). Military designations (M-[code]) are Concordat or private fleet designations — civilian access requires authorization. Offline beacons can still be used as approach references if you have old coordinates, but drift increases approach failure risk by 10% per year since last update.
